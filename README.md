Love mahjong but got no friends to play with? Or maybe your friends all suddenly become "busy" the moment you mention mahjong? 😇

If so, Welcome to the Sichuan Mahjong Simulator!

🤔 So… why did I make this?

One day I was chatting with my Indian friend. She really wanted to learn mahjong, and I really wanted to teach her.
But here comes the problem:

We are in Australia 🇦🇺 Finding FOUR people for mahjong here is basically harder than hitting all green lights on parramatta road 🚗😇

And somehow a real mahjong set costs like 100 bucks 💸💀

So I thought:

"Fine. I'll just code the whole thing myself."

And that is how this project was born 😂
